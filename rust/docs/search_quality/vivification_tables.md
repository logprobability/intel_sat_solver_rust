Generated by `scripts/vivify_bench.py`; the reading is in [`vivification.md`](vivification.md).

Runs: 2016; instances: 144; timeout: 120 s per run; every model is verified. A `-` is a run without an answer (a timeout).
The conflicts and decisions are cumulative over the queries of an instance (those of the search: vivification counts neither). The status of a recorded planner instance is that of its last query.

The configurations:

* `base`: the defaults (no inprocessing)
* `ing`: the control: `/inprocessing/on 1 /inprocessing/conflicts_before_next 5000` without vivification (inprocessing itself does nothing else in the default mode)
* `viv`: `ing` and `/inprocessing/vivify/on 1` (`/inprocessing/vivify/effort 0.1`, the default)
* `viv:e0.3`: the same with `/inprocessing/vivify/effort 0.3`
* `viv:e0.03`: the same with `/inprocessing/vivify/effort 0.03`
* `viv:e1`: the same with `/inprocessing/vivify/effort 1` (a round may propagate as much as the search has)
* `viv:n1000`: a round every 1000 conflicts (at the next restart) instead of 5000
* `viv:n20000`: a round every 20000 conflicts
* `viv:g2`: `viv` with `/inprocessing/vivify/max_glue 2`: only the clauses of the glue 2 at most
* `viv:g6`: `viv` with `/inprocessing/vivify/max_glue 6`
* `flip1000`: the control, a perturbation without information: `/decision/polarity/flip_factor 1000` (the C++ solver's own parameter: every 1000th polarity selection is flipped)
* `m1`: `/mode/value 1`, whose restarts are LBD-based: the baseline of the next two
* `m1:viv`: the mode 1 with `viv`
* `m1:viv:e0.3`: the mode 1 with `viv:e0.3`
